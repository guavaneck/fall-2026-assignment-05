Our story

A home should feel lived in, loved, and entirely yours.

We believe the things you touch every day deserve the same care as the moments they help create.

How it began

From one crowded market table

Cedar & Clay began in 2019 when friends Maya and Rowan gathered a few favorite pieces from local potters, weavers, and woodworkers at a neighborhood market. By the end of the weekend, they had found something bigger than a shop: a community that cared about where objects came from.

Today, we still choose every item the same way—with curiosity, care, and a belief that fewer, better things make room for more meaningful days.

Our mission

“To make thoughtful design feel at home—connecting people with honest materials, skilled hands, and objects worth keeping.”
